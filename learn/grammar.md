# Uro on one page

Everything here uses the default forms. The full rules, and the variants that are also correct, are in [`grammar/grammar.md`](../grammar/grammar.md). Uro is still a work in progress: the grammar and core words can change until version 1.0.

**Spelling and sound.** One letter, one sound. `k` is /k/, `s` is /s/, `z` is /ts/ or /z/, `j` is /j/ as in *yes*, `ch` is /tʃ/, `sh` is /ʃ/, `zh` is /ʒ/. Stress the second-to-last syllable: `VI-der`, `ka-LEN-dar`.

**Word order.** Subject, verb, object: `Je vider de dom.` Adjectives go before the noun and never change: `un novo dom`.

**Articles.** `un` (a) and `de` (the), the same for every noun: `un libre`, `de libre`.

**Nouns.** No gender and no cases. Plural is `-s`, or `-es` after *s*, *ks*, *z*, *sh*, *ch*: `libres`, `buses`. A woman can be marked with `-a`: `chiko`, `chika`.

**Pronouns.**

| | I | you | he | she | it | we | they |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Subject | `je` | `ju` | `on` | `ona` | `to` | `we` | `oni` |
| Object | `me` | `ju` | `on` | `ona` | `to` | `nos` | `oni` |
| Possessive | `mi` | `tu` | `su` | `su` | `su` | `nos` | `oni` |

The reflexive is `se` for everyone: `On vider se.`

**Verbs.** A verb has one form for all persons: `je vider`, `oni vider`. Verbs end in `-er` (`vider`, `parler`) or are a short root (`go`, `work`, `help`).

| Time | How | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Present | the verb | `Je work.` |
| Past | `haf` + verb | `Je haf work.` |
| Future | `wil` + verb | `Je wil work.` |
| Would | `wud` + verb | `Je wud work.` |

*To be* is `ar` and *to have* is `haf`. These three have a short past: `ared` (was), `hafed` (had), `kaned` (could).

A second verb follows directly: `Je voler go.` The helpers are `kan` (can), `mus` (must), `dever` (should), `voler` (want) and `treb` (need). Give an order with the plain verb: `Go!` *Let* is `las`: `Las nos go!`

**Not.** Put `not` before the verb: `Je not vider.` Negative words replace it: `Je vider nulting.` *Neither … nor* is `not … not`.

**Questions.**
- Yes/no: keep the order and add a question mark. `Ju parler Uro?` The answer is `ja` or `no`.
- With a question word, the verb comes next: `Wo ar de stazion?`, `Kando kom de tren?`
- If the question word is the object, keep the order: `Ki ju vider?`, `Ke ju voler?`

The question words are `ki` (who), `ke` (what), `wo` (where), `kando` (when), `komo` (how), `kuanto` (how much), `kual` (which), `pro ke` (why).

**Comparing.** `plus` (more), `de plus` (most), `min` (less), `de minst` (least), `niz` (than), `so … jak` (as … as). There are no irregular forms: `plus bon` (better), `de plus bon` (best).

```
Mi auto ar plus rapid niz tu auto.
```

**Adverbs.** Add `-li` to the adjective: `rapid`, `rapidli`. `On parler rapidli.`

**Small words.**
- Prepositions: `in`, `fon` (of), `od` (from), `a` (to), `op` (on), `mit` (with), `bez` (without), `pro` (for), `pre` (before), `post` (after), `inter` (between), `under`, `over`, `til` (until), `bi` (at, near), `za` (behind).
- Joining words: `i` (and), `o` (or), `ale` (but), `if`, `dat` (that), `ke` (which, who), `jak` (as, like), `kando` (when), `pro ke` (because).

**Numbers.** `un du tri fir fif siks set okt nin ten`, then `unten` (11), `vinti` (20), `triti` (30), `zent` (100), `mil` (1000). First is `prim`, second is `sekund`, and the rest add `-de`: `tride`.

**Making new words.** `-or` a person who does it (`work`, `workor`), `-ist` a profession (`artist`), `-zion` an action (`nazion`), `-iti` a quality (`universiti`), `-ik`, `-al` and `-iv` adjectives (`elektrik`, `nazional`, `aktiv`), `re-` again, `non-` not.
