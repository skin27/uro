# Uro 0.4 plan: the 1000 most common English words

Goal of 0.4: cover as much as possible of the
[EF top 1000 English words](https://www.ef.com/wwen/english-resources/english-vocabulary/top-1000-words/)
with Uro words, while keeping the grammar small.

---

## 1. Current coverage (after 0.3)

Each EF word was matched against the `English` column of every vocabulary table in the repository, then checked by hand.

| Status | Words | Share |
|--------|------:|------:|
| In a vocabulary table | 297 | 30% |
| Covered by grammar or word formation only (see below) | 21 | 2% |
| **Covered** | **318** | **32%** |
| Not covered | 682 | 68% |

`tools/coverage.py` (added in M0) is a little stricter, because it ignores words that only appear inside phrases. It measures **309 (30.9%)** for 0.3.0, and that is the baseline in the changelog.

**Covered by grammar only**: `a` (`un`), `be` (`ar`), `will` (`wil`), `child` (`ninio`, listed as *children*), `these` / `those` (`dis` / `dat`), `n't` (`ne`), `into` (`in`), `so` (`so`), `somebody` (`somun`), `everybody` (`al ludi`), `myself` … `themselves` (`se`), and four words that follow from existing roots: `player`, `worker`, `feeling`, `meeting`.

**Wrong matches excluded**: *early* (≠ ear), *likely* (≠ like), *nearly* (≠ near), *news* (≠ new), *shoulder* (≠ should), *training* (≠ train), *enjoy* (only in the phrase `bon apetit`).

What 0.3 covers well: pronouns, numbers, days, months, colours, family, question words, the most common prepositions and conjunctions, and about 60 core verbs.

What is missing is mostly **verbs, adjectives and abstract or domain nouns**. Roughly 40% of the missing words are international words (*information, system, problem, idea, political, modern*). The rules in [`word_formation.md`](../grammar/word_formation.md) §4 already produce these, so they are cheap to add.

### M0: Tooling
- Add `tools/coverage.py`: reads the `English` column of all tables and reports covered and missing words against a word list.
- Add the EF list as `tools/top1000.txt` with its source URL, so coverage can be measured again at the end of 0.4.

### M1: Function words (grammar)
Decisions needed first (see §4). Then:
- **Conditional**: *would, could, might*. New section in `grammar.md`; update `verbs.md`.
- **Correlatives**: extend the `som` / `al` / `nul` pattern into a full table (one / thing / place / time / way), plus the *any* series: *any, anyone, anything, another, else, whatever*. Update `demonstrative.md`.
- **Conjunctions**: *although/though, while, since, whether, nor, either … or, both … and, however, instead, thus*. Update `conjunctions.md`.
- **Prepositions**: *across, along, among, beyond, toward, within, throughout, despite, according to, including, outside, upon/onto, off*. Update `prepositions.md`.
- **Adverbs of place and time**: *up, down, away, back, ahead, ago, ever, even, just, too, quite, rather, almost, once, together, alone, well*. Update `adverbs.md`.
- **Determiners**: *same, such, own, several*.
- Add every new short word to the reserved list and to the EBNF in `grammar.md`.

### M2: Verbs
Two batches in `verbs.md`:
- **C1 everyday verbs** (about 85): *keep, let, tell, talk, turn, start, move, change, happen, seem, mean, remember, forget, follow, win, lose, grow, build, break, carry, catch, choose, fall, fly, hope, wish, worry, watch, wear, teach, throw, pull, push, sing, spend, save, cut, drive, draw, kill, fight, hit, lie, lay, hang, return, enter, fill, miss, pass, set, …*
- **C2 Latinate verbs** (about 55): *accept, admit, affect, apply, assume, avoid, compare, consider, contain, continue, control, create, decide, describe, determine, develop, discover, discuss, establish, exist, expect, explain, identify, imagine, improve, include, increase, indicate, involve, maintain, manage, mention, notice, occur, offer, perform, prepare, prevent, produce, protect, prove, provide, realize, recognize, reduce, reflect, relate, remain, remove, represent, require, respond, reveal, serve, suffer, suggest, support, treat*. These take the `-er` form of the shared root (`aksepter`, `kreater`, `deskriber`, …).
- Add a rule to `word_formation.md`: how a Latinate verb root becomes a Uro verb, so that new verbs can be made without a table.

### M3: Adjectives & adverbs
- Add the 92 adjectives to `adjectives.md`, with opposites where they exist (*high/low, dark/light, true/false, right/wrong, safe/dangerous*).
- Split off the international adjectives (*nasional, sosial, politik, publik, modern, …*) into `adjectives_international.md` if the table gets too long.
- `-ly` adverbs then follow with `-li` (`klar` → `klarli`). Only irregular ones (*well, soon, perhaps, indeed, later*) need a table row.
- Decide on *better / best* (see §4).

### M4: Everyday nouns
New tables, following the `everyday.md` / `travel.md` format:
- `basic/people.md`: people & roles (E), using `-ist` / `-or`.
- `basic/home.md`: home, things & materials (F).
- `basic/places.md`: places & directions (G), with *north, south, east, west* and *western, southern*.
- Time words (H) into `nature_time.md`.
- Body & health (I) into `food_body.md`, or a new `body_health.md`.

### M5: International nouns
Mostly direct application of `word_formation.md` §4. New tables:
- `basic/society.md` (J), `basic/work.md` (K), `basic/media.md` (L), `basic/leisure.md` (M), `basic/abstract.md` (N).

### For every milestone
- Every row gets all 16 language columns, like 0.3. Leave `Uro (AI)` as `—`.
- Check each new word against the reserved list and against existing words (as with `nos` / `nas` in 0.3).
- Spell by the rules in [`pronunciation.md`](../grammar/pronunciation.md).
- Update `README.md` (file list) and `changelog.md` (`0.4.0`).
- Run `tools/coverage.py` and record the number in the changelog.

## 4. Decisions

Made in M1 (see [`grammar.md`](../grammar/grammar.md) and [`changelog.md`](../grammar/changelog.md)):

1. **Conditional**: `wud` + bare verb, built like the future `wil`. `could` / `might` = `wud kan`.
2. **Any**: no separate series. `som` in questions and `if` clauses, `nul` with negation, `kada` for "any you like".
3. **up / down**: `op` (up, also *on*) and `ab` (down, off), like German *auf und ab* and Dutch *op en af*.
4. **better / best**: already settled in 0.3: `mer bon`, `de mest bon` (no irregular comparatives).

Still open:

5. **Scope**: M1–M5 all in 0.4, or M5 in 0.5.

Also open from 0.3 and cheap to close in 0.4: `jez`, `theater`, `whiski`, `zoo` spelling; Cyrillic letters in transliterations; `ja` vs `da`; `vinti` vs `duti`.
