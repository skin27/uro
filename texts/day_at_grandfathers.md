# Un Den bi Granpapa

*A Day at Grandfather's*, an everyday text in the present tense. Every word comes from the vocabulary tables, and every sentence follows [`grammar.md`](../grammar/grammar.md), using the default forms.

## Uro

Mi granpapa i mi granmama viver in un selo bi un lago.
Oni haf un kaval, un donki, du vakas, vari ovzas, un porko i mult pulos.
Kada matin, bi aurora, de gans buder al ludi.
Je porter star kleider i stivels, i je go a de kuchnia.
Pro brekfast, granmama friter eis i kolbasa in un pana, i mi sister mikser farina, melk i mel in un miska.
Post brekfast, je help granpapa. We go a de forest bi de holm, pro ke de stufa treb drevo.
Granpapa rezer un star dub mit un topor i un pila, i je leger de drevo op de karo.
De donki ar lazi i not voler go, ale un poko mais fakter to walk.
Bi medio den, we sit under un baum bi de strom, i we jester pan, kas i matur vinograd.
Mi pupi drink od de strom i salter in de blato.
Dan de klima kamber rapidli: grand nubes kover de sol, i we hor tonder.
Un bliz briler over de vale, i we korer a de dom, mokri i fatigado.
In de vecher, al ludi sit bi de stufa.
Granpapa dir un star histori, granmama koser un wol sok, i de pupi slap op mi futs.

## English

My grandfather and my grandmother live in a village by a lake.
They have a horse, a donkey, two cows, several sheep, a pig and many chickens.
Every morning, at dawn, the goose wakes everyone.
I wear old clothes and boots, and I go to the kitchen.
For breakfast, Grandmother fries eggs and sausage in a pan, and my sister mixes flour, milk and honey in a bowl.
After breakfast, I help Grandfather. We go to the forest by the hill, because the stove needs wood.
Grandfather cuts an old oak with an axe and a saw, and I put the wood on the cart.
The donkey is lazy and doesn't want to go, but a little corn makes it walk.
At noon, we sit under a tree by the stream, and we eat bread, cheese and ripe grapes.
My puppy drinks from the stream and jumps in the mud.
Then the weather changes quickly: big clouds cover the sun, and we hear thunder.
Lightning shines over the valley, and we run to the house, wet and tired.
In the evening, everyone sits by the stove.
Grandfather tells an old story, Grandmother sews a wool sock, and the puppy sleeps on my feet.

## Grammar notes

| Uro | Rule |
|-----|------|
| `viver`, `go`, `sit` | a verb has one form for all persons |
| `vakas`, `ovzas`, `stivels`, `futs` | plural, default (`-s`) |
| `star kleider`, `matur vinograd` | adjectives go before the noun and never change |
| `not voler go` | a second verb follows directly |
| `fakter to walk` | causative: `fakter` + object + bare verb |
| `rapidli` | adverb, default (`-li`) |
| `pro ke` | *because* |
| `wol sok` | compound, with the modifier first |
| `medio den` | transparent phrase "middle day" = noon |
