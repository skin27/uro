# Supa fon Fasol

*Bean Soup*, a recipe, to show the imperative. Every word comes from the vocabulary tables, and every sentence follows [`grammar.md`](../grammar/grammar.md), using the default forms.

## Uro

Dis supa ar simpel, plezant i sano.
Ju treb fasol, du kartofels, un poko kolbasa, sal, peper, oli i water.
Prim, laver de fasol i leger to in un pot mit kold water.
Not jester de fasol krud: krud fasol ar multi mal pro de stomak.
Buler de fasol durant un ur.
Rezer de kartofels i de kolbasa in malen stuks mit un sharp kniv.
Friter de kolbasa in un pana mit un poko oli, til to ar braun.
Dan ader de kartofels i de kolbasa a de pot, i mikser al mit un spun.
Taster de supa. If to ar zu slani, ader un poko water.
Leger de supa in miskas, i dar pan i buter a al ludi.
Bon apetit!

## English

This soup is simple, pleasant and healthy.
You need beans, two potatoes, a little sausage, salt, pepper, oil and water.
First, wash the beans and put them in a pot with cold water.
Don't eat the beans raw: raw beans are very bad for the stomach.
Boil the beans for an hour.
Cut the potatoes and the sausage into small pieces with a sharp knife.
Fry the sausage in a pan with a little oil, until it is brown.
Then add the potatoes and the sausage to the pot, and mix everything with a spoon.
Taste the soup. If it is too salty, add a little water.
Put the soup in bowls, and give bread and butter to everyone.
Enjoy your meal!

## Grammar notes

| Uro | Rule |
|-----|------|
| `laver`, `buler`, `rezer`, `ader` | imperative: the base form, with no subject |
| `Not jester` | a negative order: `not` before the verb |
| `ju treb` | `treb` means *need* |
| `fasol` | a mass word, used as a singular, so it takes `to` (it) |
| `in malen stuks` | plural, default (`-s`) |
| `If to ar zu slani` | a real condition takes the present |
| `durant un ur` | `durant` means *during* or, with a length of time, *for* |
