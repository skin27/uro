# De Wind i de Sol

A short retelling of Aesop's fable *The North Wind and the Sun*, the classic test text for languages. Every word comes from the vocabulary tables, and every sentence follows [`grammar.md`](../grammar/grammar.md) (v0.3).

## Uro

De wind i de sol demandered: "Ki fon we ar mer sterk?"
Then un man komed op de weg, in un warm mantel.
De wind i de sol saged: "De prim ke fakter de man open su mantel, ar de mest sterk."
De wind blased so sterk jak on kan, ma de man holded su mantel mer sterk rund se.
Then de wind stoped.
Then de sol komed, warm i bel, i de man direkt opened su mantel.
Pro dis, de wind haf sag ke de sol ar de mest sterk fon de du.

## English

The wind and the sun asked: "Who of us is stronger?"
Then a man came along the road, in a warm coat.
The wind and the sun said: "The first who makes the man open his coat is the strongest."
The wind blew as hard as he could, but the man held his coat tighter around himself.
Then the wind stopped.
Then the sun came, warm and beautiful, and the man opened his coat at once.
Therefore the wind said that the sun was the strongest of the two.

## Grammar notes

| Uro | Rule |
|-----|------|
| `demandered`, `komed`, `blased`, `stoped` | past tense, variant 2 (`-ed`) |
| `haf sag` | past tense, variant 1 (`haf` + verb) |
| `Ki fon we` | question word `ki` + preposition `fon` (of) + `we` |
| `mer sterk` / `de mest sterk` | analytic comparison |
| `so sterk jak` | equality: *so … jak* |
| `de prim ke …` | relative clause with the universal `ke` |
| `fakter de man open` | causative: `fakter` + object + bare verb |
| `rund se` | reflexive `se` after a preposition |
| `Pro dis` | transparent phrase "for this" = therefore |
